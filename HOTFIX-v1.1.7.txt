MONSTRUM CODEX INFINITE v1.1.7 OFFICIAL MEDIA REPAIR HOTFIX

1. Close Monstrum Codex and the dedicated cache browser.
2. Extract this archive beside your current installation.
3. Merge and replace files inside Monstrum_Codex_Infinite.
4. Keep your existing userdata folder.
5. Launch the app and press Ctrl+F5 once. Existing portrait or entry media can now fill previously blank cards immediately.
6. Run cache_official_images_windows.bat normally to repair unresolved mugshots and re-index compact/unnumbered portrait filenames.

Do not use --refresh unless you deliberately want to redownload every media asset. Schema v3 rechecks old manifests while reusing healthy local files.
